Help
Why can't I see a section?
Some sections — Portfolio Analytics and Management — are limited by rank. Ask a Head or Lead Designer if you need access.
How do pending submissions work?
Designer and Assistant Lead uploads go to Pending Approval first. Lead and Head Designer can approve or reject from there.
A page won't load or looks stuck
Use the refresh button in that section's header first. If the page truly hangs, a red error banner will appear at the bottom of the screen — copy it and send it to whoever maintains this dashboard.
